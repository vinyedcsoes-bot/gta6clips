Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

GTA 6 CLIPS ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

The app uses the TikTok Content Posting API and Login Kit. Use of TikTok is subject to the TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Information the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app receives an OAuth token with these permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos and set their title, description, tags, privacy and publish time.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the name and ID of the signed-in channel, so the owner can confirm the right channel.

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token with these permissions:

  • user.info.basic: to read the display name and avatar of the signed-in TikTok account, so the owner can confirm the right account.
  • video.upload: to send a video to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft.
  • video.publish: to publish a video directly to the owner's TikTok account with the caption and privacy level the owner chose.

How the information is used, stored and shared

  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in local files. They are never sent anywhere except to Google or TikTok.
  • Upload results (video or publish ID, link and status) are saved in local files on the owner's computer.
  • The app does not collect, store or share data about any other person, including viewers, commenters or other accounts.
  • The app does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.
  • The app does not use cookies, analytics or advertising, and does not place anything on users' devices.

Revoking access and deleting data

Google access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. TikTok access can be revoked in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. To delete all stored data, the owner deletes the local token and result files. Data is deleted within 7 days of revocation, or immediately on request.
