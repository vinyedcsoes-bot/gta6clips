GTA 6 CLIPS

GTA 6 CLIPS is a private desktop publishing tool used only by its owner. It uploads short gameplay clips that the owner edited to the owner's own accounts on YouTube and TikTok, with title, caption, hashtags and a publish time. The main account publishes in Brazilian Portuguese ("GTA 6 CLIP BRASIL"); a separate English-language account may be added later.

  • It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
  • It only accesses the owner's own accounts, after the owner signs in with Google (OAuth 2.0) or with TikTok (Login Kit).
  • Every clip is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is sent.
  • It uses YouTube API Services and the TikTok Content Posting API.

How it works

  1. The owner edits a clip and writes its title, caption and hashtags in a local queue file.
  2. The owner marks the clip as approved.
  3. The tool sends the approved clip to the owner's account: on TikTok, either to the TikTok inbox as a draft (the owner finishes and posts it in the TikTok app) or as a direct post with the privacy level the owner chose.
